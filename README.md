# 🚗 Driven – AI-Assisted Smart Dispatch & Rider Platform

![Driven Logo](path/to/logo.png)

**Driven** is an **AI-powered, intuitive dispatch platform** designed to bring **efficiency, intuition, and human-centered coordination** to urban mobility. It empowers drivers, riders, and independent operators to collaborate seamlessly while optimizing safety, fairness, and operational intelligence.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🤖 AI-Assisted Dispatching:**  
  Dynamically optimizes rides, routes, and scheduling using real-time and historical data while preserving driver autonomy.

- **🗺️ Full Mapping & Traffic Intelligence:**  
  Integrates maps, live traffic, hotspots, and route optimization for safe, efficient navigation.

- **☔ Weather & Environmental Adaptation:**  
  Adjusts routing based on real-time weather and environmental conditions.

- **⏰ Time-Zone & Event Awareness:**  
  Considers local peak periods, events, and temporal patterns to reduce idle time and anticipate demand.

- **✊ Union-Friendly & Human-Centered:**  
  Supports independent operators and small fleets with cooperative, transparent systems.

- **📊 Data-Driven Insights & Analytics:**  
  Provides predictive trends, actionable intelligence, and adaptive learning for drivers and fleet managers.
